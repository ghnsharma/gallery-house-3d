# Gallery House · Akar Design
Luxury floor-plan-to-3D showcase. Static three.js site (no build step, three.js from CDN).

- **Scroll story** (default): a fixed full-screen 3D canvas; scroll drives the camera along CatmullRom splines (position + look-at) from an aerial hero shot through the entry court, foyer, gallery, dining/kitchen, library, primary suite, garden court and living room, out to the infinity pool. Chapter nav on the right jumps to sections.
- **Explore freely**: dollhouse orbit (roof hidden, room labels), first-person walk (WASD/arrows, mouse look, Shift run, mobile joystick), clickable minimap, auto tour.
- Rendering: ACES tone mapping, sRGB, PMREM sky environment, soft shadows, bloom on desktop (disabled with lower pixel ratio on mobile; force with `?lite` / `?nobloom`).
